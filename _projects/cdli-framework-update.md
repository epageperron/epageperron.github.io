- **Role:** Project Manager and acting Principal Investigator, UCLA, with Robert K. Englund
- **Funding:** National Endowment for the Humanities, grant [HAA-256224-17](https://awardsearch.neh.gov/AwardDetail.aspx?gn=HAA-256224-17), about US$140,000

The project rebuilt the CDLI on a modular, open-source framework ([GitLab](https://gitlab.com/cdli/framework)) with an API, converters and validators for cuneiform data formats (ATF, TEI, CoNLL, Brat), expanded [documentation](https://cdli.earth/docs), and Docker-based deployment mirrored at partner institutions. The interface was designed for accessibility and shaped by user surveys and testing, in close collaboration with the [MTAAC](/projects/mtaac/) team in Toronto.

The new platform underpins all later CDLI work. In 2024 the site reached 2 million views, ten times its earlier traffic.
