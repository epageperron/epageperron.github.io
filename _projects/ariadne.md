- **Role:** Research Associate, Archaeology Data Service
- **Infrastructure:** [ARIADNE Research Infrastructure AISBL](https://ariadne-infrastructure.eu/), the non-profit association under Belgian law that carries on the ARIADNE and ARIADNEplus projects funded by the European Commission

The [ARIADNE portal](https://portal.ariadne-infrastructure.eu/) aggregates more than four million archaeological resources from partners across Europe into a single catalogue, searchable by place, period and subject. Émilie works on the ingestion and mapping of partner datasets to the [AO-Cat ontology](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15268834), the CIDOC CRM based model behind the catalogue, and on bringing records of inscribed artefacts from the [CDLI](https://cdli.earth/) into the portal so that cuneiform texts can be found alongside the archaeology of their sites.
