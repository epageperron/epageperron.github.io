- **Role:** Archaeological Database Manager, 2013–2016
- **Institutions:** University of Toronto and the Oriental Institute of the University of Chicago
- **Director:** Clemens Reichel

Between 1930 and 1937 the Oriental Institute excavated Khafajah, Tell Asmar, Tell Agrab and Ishchali in the Diyala river basin north-east of Baghdad. The architecture and some classes of finds were published in monographs, but most of the some 15,000 recorded objects were not. The Diyala Project publishes them online, with the excavation records, in a searchable relational database. Émilie managed that database and built a [map interface](https://github.com/Diyala-OI/interface) for navigating the small finds.
