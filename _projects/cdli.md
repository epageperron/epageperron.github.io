- **Website:** [cdli.earth](https://cdli.earth/)
- **Role:** Co-Director since 2020; Co-Principal Investigator 2016–2020; contributor since 2011
- **Partners:** University of Oxford, Max Planck Institute of Geoanthropology (Jena), University of York

The [CDLI](https://cdli.earth/) catalogues, images and transliterates the world's cuneiform artefacts, some 400,000 to date, and publishes them openly with an API, data dumps and a knowledge graph. As Co-Director, Émilie is responsible for the platform, data standards and access.

She is Editor in Chief of the [Cuneiform Digital Library Notes](https://cdli.earth/postings/199) and a member of the editorial board of the Cuneiform Digital Library Journal and Bulletin. She has administered and mentored the CDLI's Google Summer of Code programme since 2018.
