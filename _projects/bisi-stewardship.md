- **Role:** Co-Principal Investigator
- **Funding:** British Institute for the Study of Iraq, £8,880, 2026–2027

The pilot combines the care of cuneiform collections in Iraq with the edition and open digital publication of their texts, as a first step towards a network of Iraqi and international partners. It extends the collaboration with Iraqi universities begun in [CDLI-ACT](/projects/cdli-act/) and continued in [Towards Universal Access to Cuneiform Heritage](/projects/universal-access/).
