- **Role:** Researcher Co-Investigator, with Christie Carr and Jacob L. Dahl (University of Oxford)
- **Funding:** John Fell Fund, £52,000, 2024–2025

The project asks what cuneiform texts can tell us about human impact on the environment of southern Iraq in the third millennium BCE, taking fisheries as the test case. Administrative, legal and epistolary texts from Girsu, Umma and Iri-Sagrig record fish rations, shortages, fish ponds and managed breeding. Read against archaeological and palaeoenvironmental evidence, they show early signs of both over-exploitation and attempts at sustainability in a wetland that was again devastated in the 1990s.

**Outputs**

- Workshop _Writing the Dawn of the Anthropocene_, Rencontre Assyriologique Internationale 70, Prague, July 2025 (with Christie Carr and Rune Rattenborg).
- Carr, Pagé-Perron and Dahl, _Human-Environment Interactions at the Dawn of the Anthropocene: A Case Study of Third-Millennium Fisheries in South-Western Asia_ (in preparation).
