Software, Websites and Datasets (25)

  • CDLI platform (4)
    • cdli.earth, the Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative website. Lead.
    • CDLI framework, the open-source platform behind the site, in active development since 2017. Lead.
    • Documentation and knowledge graph with SPARQL endpoint. Team.
    • API client and data releases, the daily catalogue and text dumps now served through the API. Lead on data, team on client.
  • MTAAC outputs, Sumerian natural language processing (2017–2021) (5)
    • Sumerian translation pipeline: part-of-speech tagging, named-entity recognition and machine translation for Ur III texts. Team.
    • Semi-supervised NMT for Sumerian–English and neural machine translation experiments. Mentor.
    • Gold corpus, dependency syntax corpus and Ur III corpus of manually annotated Sumerian. Team.
    • Annotation tools: atf2conll, morphology pre-annotation, annotation assistant, Brat to CoNLL converter, conllu.py. Team and mentor.
    • Project website and work packages. Lead.
  • Research tools (4)
    • conll2graphml, network graphs of people co-occurring in texts, used for the Adab prosopography. Lead.
    • atf2conc, text preparation for concordance software. Lead.
    • cuneiform_mining, database and interface to collect, standardise, mine and visualise cuneiform data. Lead.
    • UrIIIPro, Ur III provenience classification in R. Lead.
  • Vocabularies and ontologies (2)
    • GLoW vocabularies for inscribed artefact metadata. Team.
    • AO-Cat ontology, the CIDOC CRM based ontology of the ARIADNE catalogue. Team.
  • Archaeological data infrastructure (3)
    • ARIADNE portal, ingestion and mapping of partner datasets, including CDLI records. Team.
    • welsh-trusts-greylit, ingest of Welsh Trusts grey literature into the ADS Library. Lead.
    • Diyala interface, map-based navigation of the Diyala Project small finds. Lead.
  • Mentored projects (Google Summer of Code and students, 2018–2022) (7)
    • Cuneiform OCR, line and sign detection on tablet images.
    • CDLI mobile app and tablet app.
    • pyoracc, Python tools for working with ATF.
    • coco-convertor, converts COCO image annotations to the open annotation format.
    • Publication entity extractor, finds CDLI entities in publication PDFs.
    • Publication merging, de-duplication of bibliographic records.
    • Temporal data schema, granular temporal data management for the CDLI.
