ATRIUM

Horizon Europe project bridging the ARIADNE, DARIAH, CLARIN and OPERAS infrastructures. Émilie leads the demonstrators work at the Archaeology Data Service.

  • Role: Research Associate, Archaeology Data Service
  • Project: ATRIUM, Advancing Frontier Research in the Arts and Humanities, funded by the European Commission, 2024–2027

ATRIUM connects four European research infrastructures, ARIADNE for archaeology, DARIAH and CLARIN for the humanities and language technologies, and OPERAS for open scholarly communication, so that researchers can move data and methods between them. The Archaeology Data Service builds demonstrators that show these connections working on real research questions. Émilie’s work focuses on place-based linking and visualisation of multidisciplinary research data, and on reusable, replicable digital workflows for archaeology.
